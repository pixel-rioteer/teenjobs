Simple by design

How Bobajob works.

Every job follows a clear process, with admin review and a parent or guardian involved before the job is confirmed.

1

An adult posts a job

They add a clear description, fixed price, estimated duration, date, time, approximate location and tags.

2

Bobajob reviews it

An administrator checks the listing against the platform's rules before it becomes visible.

3

A teenager requests it

The teenager chooses a suitable listing. Their parent or guardian must approve that specific job.

4

Everyone stays connected

Once approvals are complete, the adult, teenager and parent join one job chat to confirm arrangements.

5

Both confirm completion

The adult and teenager separately confirm the job is finished. Disagreements are handled before payment release.

6

Both leave a review

The adult and teenager can leave a rating and feedback after the job has been marked complete.

The live service must first confirm the rules for child work, minimum ages, permitted tasks, insurance, identity checks and payments. This preview does not authorise anyone to undertake work.
Designed with care

Safety comes first.

Bobajob is intended to connect adults with young people. The live service must have robust safeguards before it launches.

👪

Parent approval

A teenager must link a parent or guardian account. Each accepted job requires a separate parent approval.

🛡️

Admin-reviewed listings

Every job listing must be reviewed before it is shown to teenagers. Unsafe or unsuitable tasks are rejected.

🔒

Private profiles

Teenagers use usernames publicly. Real names, contact details and precise home locations are not public.

💬

Three-person job chat

The adult, teenager and linked parent join the same conversation after the required approvals.

🚩

Reporting and moderation

Users can report listings, accounts and messages. Reports go to an administrator for review.

⚖️

Age-appropriate work

Permitted ages, tasks and working hours must be checked against applicable UK child employment rules.

Important: Bobajob is a concept preview, not a live or verified safeguarding service. Do not use it to arrange work or share personal information.
